# Image prompts for the /hire pages

Use with Midjourney, DALL·E, Imagen or Flux. Export 1200×630 (OG/social) and 1600×900 (page hero) as WebP, then save in `public/services/<slug>-og.webp`. Style anchor for all three: editorial, flat-shaded 3D-meets-print look, matte textures, generous negative space, palette of electric blue `#0145fd`, soft pink `#fedff5` and near-black. No text, no logos, no faces.

Negative prompt (all): text, watermark, logo, human face, stock-photo look, clutter, neon cyberpunk, glossy plastic.

## 1. hire-software-engineer-india
A lone laptop on a minimal desk seen from above at an angle, a clean code editor glowing softly on its screen, a thin glowing line stretching from the laptop across an abstract map silhouette of India toward a distant globe, electric blue and soft pink accents on a near-black background, editorial flat-shaded 3D, matte textures, wide negative space on the left for a headline, 16:9.

## 2. hire-react-nextjs-developer-india
Floating translucent UI components (a button, a card, a toggle, a nav bar) assembled like building blocks into a tidy interface, an abstract atom-like orbit ring behind them, electric blue and soft pink on near-black, crisp edge lighting, minimalist editorial flat-shaded 3D, matte, centered composition with breathing room, 16:9.

## 3. hire-forward-deployed-engineer-india
Two abstract scenes joined by a bridge of glowing blue nodes: on the left a stylised product dashboard, on the right a stylised customer workspace with sticky notes and a whiteboard, a small paper-plane figure travelling along the bridge, electric blue and soft pink on near-black, editorial flat-shaded 3D, matte textures, wide 16:9.

## Optional extras
- **Portrait treatment (about section):** studio portrait of a software engineer at a desk, soft key light, electric-blue rim light, shallow depth of field. Generate from your own photo rather than a synthetic face.
- **Section icons (6):** isometric matte icons for frontend, backend, AI, blockchain, cloud and leadership, same palette, transparent background, 512×512.
